# Password Issues

## 🔐 Flippi Uses Passwordless Authentication

Flippi uses **Google Sign-In** exclusively - no passwords required!

### How to Log In

1. Click **"Sign in with Google"** button
2. Select your Google account
3. You're logged in - no password needed!

### Benefits of Passwordless

- ✅ **No forgotten passwords** - Ever!
- ✅ **More secure** - Google handles security
- ✅ **Faster login** - One click access
- ✅ **No password resets** - Nothing to reset

### Common Login Issues

#### Can't See Google Sign-In Button

- Clear browser cache (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows)
- Try a different browser
- Disable ad blockers temporarily
- Check JavaScript is enabled

#### Google Account Issues

If you can't sign in with Google:
- Verify your Google account is active
- Try signing into gmail.com first
- Use a different Google account
- Clear Google cookies

#### "Authentication Failed" Error

This can happen when:
- Your session expired (after 30 days)
- Cookies are blocked
- Using private/incognito mode

**Fix**: Clear cookies and try again

### Account Access Problems

#### Lost Access to Google Account

If you lose access to your Google account:
1. Recover your Google account at https://accounts.google.com/recovery
2. Once recovered, you can log into Flippi again
3. Your Flippi data remains safe

#### Want to Use Different Google Account

Currently logged in with wrong account?
1. Click your profile icon
2. Select "Sign Out"
3. Sign in with correct Google account

### Session & Security

- Sessions last **30 days**
- Automatic logout after 30 days of inactivity
- Secure HTTPOnly cookies used
- HTTPS encryption on all environments

## 🆘 Need Help?

Since we don't use passwords, most issues are Google-related:

1. First try: Clear cookies and cache
2. Check your Google account works
3. Contact support if issues persist: support@flippi.ai
