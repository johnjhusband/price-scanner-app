# Account Deletion

## 🗑️ How to Delete Your Account

### Current Process

To delete your Flippi account and all associated data:

1. **Email Request**
   - Send email to: teamflippi@gmail.com
   - Subject: "Account Deletion Request"
   - Include your account email address
   - We'll process within 30 days

2. **What We Need**
   - Email address used for sign-in
   - Confirmation you want ALL data deleted
   - Any specific concerns or requests

### What Gets Deleted

When you request account deletion, we remove:

✅ **Account Information**
- Name and email
- Google account link
- Profile picture

✅ **Activity Data**
- Scan history
- Item analysis results
- Usage statistics

✅ **Subscription Data**
- Payment history
- Subscription status
- Billing information

✅ **Feedback**
- Any feedback submitted
- Support tickets

### Before You Delete

#### Consider These Alternatives

**Taking a Break?**
- Just sign out - your account remains but inactive
- No emails or notifications
- Return anytime with same Google account

**Privacy Concerns?**
- We don't sell or share your data
- Images aren't stored after processing
- Review our [Privacy Policy](https://app.flippi.ai/privacy)

**Having Issues?**
- Contact support first: teamflippi@gmail.com
- We might be able to help!

#### What You'll Lose

⚠️ **Permanent Deletion**
- Cannot recover account after deletion
- Lose all scan history
- Lose any remaining credits
- Must create new account to use again

### Deletion Timeline

1. **Day 0**: You email request
2. **Day 1-2**: We confirm receipt
3. **Day 3-29**: Processing period
4. **Day 30**: Account fully deleted
5. **Day 31+**: All data permanently removed

### Data Retention

Per our privacy policy:
- Account deleted within 30 days
- Some data may be retained for legal compliance
- Anonymized analytics may remain
- No personal info kept after deletion

### Your Rights

Under privacy laws, you have the right to:
- Request account deletion
- Receive copy of your data
- Know what data we collect
- Opt out of communications

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Can I cancel deletion request?
Yes, email us before the 30-day period ends.

#### What if I used multiple emails?
List all emails in your request.

#### Can I delete just some data?
Currently it's all or nothing. We're working on selective deletion.

#### Will you confirm deletion?
Yes, we'll email confirmation when complete.

#### What about subscription refunds?
- Monthly: No refund for partial month
- Credits: No refund for unused credits
- Contact billing for special circumstances

#### Can I export my data first?
Not yet automated. Request data export in your email.

### After Deletion

#### What Happens
- Can't sign in with deleted account
- Email address can be reused
- Must create new account from scratch

#### If You Change Your Mind
- Can't restore deleted account
- Can create new account
- Will start fresh with no history
